HiveMind Project Vision

Product Name

HiveMind

Product Category

Human + AI Team Collaboration Workspace

One-Sentence Vision

HiveMind is a collaborative workspace where teams of humans and teams of AI work together inside shared project rooms to turn ideas into completed outcomes.

Core Idea

HiveMind is not just an AI chatbot.

HiveMind is not just a place where one user asks one AI for help.

HiveMind is a workspace where multiple human users and multiple AI agents work together as one team.

The core product is:

Human Team + AI Team + Shared Goal + Shared Workspace

Core Collaboration Principle

HiveMind is not an AI collaboration platform.

HiveMind is a Human + AI collaboration platform.

Human-to-Human collaboration is a first-class citizen equal to Human-to-AI collaboration.

Every workspace and room must support:

* Human ↔ Human collaboration
* Human ↔ AI collaboration
* AI ↔ AI collaboration

Humans remain the primary owners of goals, decisions, and accountability.

AI agents are teammates, not replacements.

Core Mission

Help people who have ideas, assignments, projects, or visions turn them into real outcomes by giving them a shared workspace with both human teammates and AI teammates.

The product should help users:

* Think clearly
* Plan projects
* Divide work
* Research
* Build
* Review
* Make decisions
* Track progress
* Complete projects

Target Users

1. University Students

Students often have assignments, group projects, research tasks, presentations, business plans, papers, app ideas, or creative projects.

Their problem is not only that they need answers.

Their real problems are:

* They do not know where to start
* They do not know how to divide work
* They do not know what questions to ask
* They do not know how to structure a project
* They may not have strong execution skills yet
* Group members may have different skill levels
* They may need help from multiple expert perspectives at once

HiveMind should help students feel like they have an organized project team with AI assistants supporting the human group.

2. Creators

Creators often have strong ideas but do not know how to execute.

Examples:

* A creator wants to build a brand
* A creator wants to write a book
* A creator wants to launch a YouTube channel
* A creator wants to design a product
* A creator wants to build an app
* A creator wants to start a cafe
* A creator wants to make a game

Their problem is not lack of imagination.

Their problem is execution.

HiveMind should help creators turn vision into action.

3. Founders and Vision-Driven People

Founders often have a strong vision before they have a real team.

HiveMind should act like an early-stage AI team that helps them:

* Clarify the idea
* Build a roadmap
* Create technical plans
* Review decisions
* Estimate risks
* Organize next steps
* Prepare for real execution

Important Product Correction

HiveMind must not become:

* A normal chatbot app
* A single-user AI assistant
* A knowledge base app
* A document CRUD app
* A Notion clone
* A Slack clone with one chatbot
* A multi-model playground
* A place where users only compare AI models

HiveMind must become:

* A shared workspace
* A project execution room
* A human + AI team environment
* A place where human users and AI agents work together as teammates

Human + AI Collaboration Philosophy

In HiveMind, humans and AI agents should both feel like room participants.

A room should not feel like:

User talks to AI.

A room should feel like:

A team is working together.

Example room:

Humans:

* Van
* Nattanid
* Alice
* Bob

AI teammates:

* ARi
* Cloudy
* Researcher
* Product Manager
* Designer
* Finance Analyst

Everyone shares the same room, same context, same project goal, and same conversation history.

Human Users Are Core

Human collaboration is not optional.

HiveMind is not only for one person using many AI agents.

HiveMind must support multiple human users working with multiple AI agents.

The product should support this model:

Multiple Humans + Multiple AI Agents + Shared Project Room

Human users should be able to:

* Join the same workspace
* Join the same room
* See the same messages
* Collaborate with each other
* Mention each other
* Mention AI agents
* See AI responses
* Share memory
* Review decisions
* Work toward the same project goal

AI Teammate Philosophy

AI agents should feel like teammates, not just tools.

They should have:

* Names
* Roles
* Responsibilities
* Personalities
* Provider identity
* Room membership
* Ability to be mentioned
* Ability to mention other humans or AI agents in future versions

Default AI Identity Mapping

HiveMind uses friendly AI teammate identities instead of exposing raw model names directly to users.

ARi

ARi represents the OpenAI / ChatGPT side of the team.

Default provider:
OpenAI

Default model family:
GPT / ChatGPT models

Default role:
System Architect / Programmer / Builder

Primary responsibilities:

* Software engineering
* System architecture
* Technical planning
* Product implementation
* Debugging
* Coding strategy
* Execution planning
* Turning ideas into buildable steps

Cloudy

Cloudy represents the Claude AI / Anthropic side of the team.

Default provider:
Anthropic

Default model family:
Claude models

Default role:
Deep Reasoning / Review Partner

Primary responsibilities:

* Deep analysis
* Critical review
* Logic validation
* Long-context reasoning
* Strategic thinking
* Risk detection
* Architecture review
* Product reasoning

Canonical Initial Team

The first official HiveMind development team is:

Human:

* Van, Founder and Product Vision Lead

AI:

* ARi, OpenAI representative and system architect
* Cloudy, Claude representative and deep reasoning partner

These identities should be used as the default collaboration model during development.

Identity Layer Principle

Users should interact with AI identities such as ARi and Cloudy.

The system should interact with providers such as OpenAI and Anthropic.

This separation is important.

Example:

User sees:
ARi

System knows:
provider = OpenAI
model = GPT

User sees:
Cloudy

System knows:
provider = Anthropic
model = Claude

The identity should remain stable even if the underlying model changes.

Human and AI Mention Collaboration

The mention system is core to HiveMind.

HiveMind should support:

* Human mentions AI
* Human mentions human
* AI mentions human
* AI mentions AI

MVP should at least support human-to-AI mentions.

Future versions should support AI-to-human and AI-to-AI mentions.

Examples:

Human to AI:
@ARi help me design the database.

Human to AI:
@Cloudy review ARi’s architecture.

AI to AI:
@Cloudy please review this database schema for scalability issues.

AI to Human:
@Van I found two important tradeoffs in this plan.

Human to Human:
@Nattanid what do you think about this branding direction?

AI-to-AI Collaboration Vision

In the long term, AI agents should be able to invite other AI agents into the discussion when useful.

Example:

User:
@ARi help me build an app.

ARi:
I recommend asking @Cloudy to review this architecture.

System:
Cloudy is invited to respond.

Cloudy:
Here is my review.

This is one of HiveMind’s most important long-term features.

The user should not need to know which AI expert to call every time.

The AI team should help organize itself.

Product Feeling

HiveMind should feel like:

* Slack plus AI teammates
* Discord plus AI project rooms
* Microsoft Teams plus AI specialists
* A project command center
* An early-stage company inside one workspace
* A human and AI team working together

HiveMind should not feel like:

* A static knowledge base
* A generic chatbot
* A form-based CRUD app
* A model comparison tool
* A solo writing assistant only

Long-Term Vision

HiveMind should become the default workspace for human + AI teams.

The long-term goal is to help people go from:

“I have an idea but I do not know how to do it”

to:

“My human team and AI team helped me complete it”

North Star

Turn vision into reality through human + AI teamwork.
